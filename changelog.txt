# 1.4.6

Added:

* Added Blue Portal Fluid as a new, cheaper Portal Fluid variant.
* Added Blue Portal Fluid cartridges.
* Added Empty Blue Portal Fluid cartridges.
* Added a Portal Gun variant containing Blue Portal Fluid.
* Added dedicated Blue Portal Fluid textures for the Portal Gun, including the Portal Gun glass, body details, cartridges, empty cartridges and projectiles.
* Added a dedicated blue portal effect for Blue Portal Fluid portals.
* Added dedicated blue projectile visuals for Blue Portal Fluid.
* Added dedicated Blue Portal Fluid Destination Wheel textures.
* Added wheel_blue.png for the Blue Portal Fluid Destination Wheel.
* Added wheel_hilight_blue.png for the Blue Portal Fluid Destination Wheel highlight.
* Added Blue Portal Fluid versions of the Portal Gun, Blue Portal Fluid cartridge and Empty Blue Portal Fluid cartridge to the Creative inventory.
* Added Blue Portal Fluid refilling from water blocks.
* Added Blue Portal Fluid refilling from water buckets.
* Added Blue Portal Fluid refilling from water bottles.
* Added Blue Portal Fluid refilling from potions.
* Added Blue Portal Fluid refilling from splash potions.
* Added Blue Portal Fluid refilling from lingering potions.
* Added the ability to refill Blue Portal Fluid directly from a water bucket in the player's inventory.
* Added automatic replacement of consumed water buckets with empty buckets when refilling Blue Portal Fluid.
* Added the "No water in inventory" message when attempting to refill Blue Portal Fluid without a water source.
* Added a recipe for Empty Blue Portal Fluid cartridges using one glass block above one empty bucket.
* Added the ability to create portals in mid-air.
* Added Portal Gun support for creating and using air portals.
* Added a new keybind for Portal Gun functionality.
* Added glowing portal rendering.
* Added blue warning messages for Blue Portal Fluid destination restrictions.
* Added the "THIS DESTINATION IS IN ANOTHER DIMENSION!" message for inaccessible saved destinations.
* Added the "THAT PLAYER IS IN ANOTHER DIMENSION!" message when attempting to target a player in another dimension.
* Added the "BLUE PORTAL FLUID IS NOT COMPATIBLE WITH INTERDIMENSIONAL TRAVEL!" message for Blue Portal Fluid dimension-listing attempts.


Changed:

* Changed Portal Gun and cartridge insertion crafting to shapeless recipes.
* Changed Portal Gun and cartridge insertion so the ingredients can be placed in any crafting position.
* Changed Portal Gun and cartridge insertion so the recipe works in both the player's inventory and a crafting table.
* Changed the Green Portal Gun's texture to match the style of the new Blue Portal Gun texture.

* Changed the Custom Destination interface for Blue Portal Fluid to display "Custom Destination: X;Y;Z" instead of the interdimensional coordinate format.
* Changed the Custom Destination interface so the "Press ? to list available dimensions" prompt is removed when using Blue Portal Fluid.
* Changed the dimension-listing function for Blue Portal Fluid so it displays "BLUE PORTAL FLUID IS NOT COMPATIBLE WITH INTERDIMENSIONAL TRAVEL!" instead of listing dimensions.
* Changed portals to glow, improving portal visibility.
