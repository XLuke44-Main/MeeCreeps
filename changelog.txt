# 1.4.5

* Changed dimension list colouring:
  * Available dimensions are now shown in aqua
  * Your current dimension is highlighted in light green
  * The "Available dimensions" header is shown in gold

* Added the ability to access the Custom Destinations menu while holding the Portal Gun
* Added the ability to open the Portal Destinations menu without needing to click on a block
* Shift-right-clicking the Portal Gun now opens the Portal Destinations menu directly
