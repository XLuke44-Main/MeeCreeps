# MeeCreeps 1.4.7.4

## Fixed

* Fixed Blue Portal Fluid cartridge refilling to recognize water buckets, regular water bottles, splash water bottles, and lingering water bottles for crafting and inventory right-click refilling.

* Fixed direct Blue Portal Fluid cartridge refilling when the water source is the water block adjacent to the clicked block face.

* Fixed an issue where portal guns could close portals opened by other portal guns via manual right-clicking.

## Changed

* Changed the cartridge crafting recipes.

* Renamed Portal Fluid to Transport Solutions for consistency.

* Updated the project to Forge 14.23.5.2859.

* Updated compilation dependencies.

* Changed the minimum required McJtyLib version from 3.5.0 to 3.5.4.

## Added

* Added Extradimensional Transport Solutions.

* Added Hyperdimensional Transport Solutions.

* Added separate configuration files for all Transport Solutions.

* Added admin-only command `/close_all_portals` to force-close all open portals.
