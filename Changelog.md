# MeeCreeps 1.4.8.3

* Fixed portal rendering issues with chests, signs, water, and connected textures.
* Fixed portal spawner portals appearing too large.
* Fixed player movement and view direction when travelling through portals with different orientations.
* Fixed portal spawner right-click behaviour.
* Fixed the manual right-click hitbox size for portals.
* Fixed Shift-right-clicking a portal spawner now opens the portal gun ui.
* Portal spawners can no longer have their active destination changed.
* Portals created by portal spawners cannot be manually closed.
* Changed the creative tab icon to Extradimensional filled Portal Gun without a durability bar.
